Biohub Rapid Response mNGS Protocols


The purpose of this document is to create a centralized location for protocols used in metagenomics sequencing (mNGS) for pathogen detection. These are guidelines for best practices and are subject to changes or modifications due to different sample types or experimental differences. Please contact the Rapid Response Team for additional questions or explanations.


For protocol revision history, please refer to the Document Revision History section at the end of this document.

1. Sample Collection, storage and preparation


Goal: To obtain a sample that will retain the integrity of nucleic acids.


Workflow:

  1. Collect samples using sterile techniques and minimizing environmental contamination, into a nuclease-free container.

  2. Store samples in DNA/RNA Shield if cold-chain is not reliable.

  3. Freeze samples at -80°C until ready to process.


Considerations:

  • Nucleic acid degradation can come from many places (DNAses, RNAses, heat, freeze/thaws, metal ions, contaminating microbes). Flash freezing is the preferred choice (liquid nitrogen or ethanol & dry ice bath).

  • RNA is more fragile than DNA

  • Preserve samples by using nucleic acid stabilizers (i.e.: DNA/RNA shield, RNAlater)


Protocols by sample type: This section covers our recommendations for sample preparation required prior to nucleic acid purification (Section 2).


Stool:

  1. Scoop ~0.2g or 200uL of sample into a sterile collection tube. Add 400uL of 1x Zymo DNA/RNA Shield to completely submerge the sample and shake to mix. Alternatively, add directly to DNA/RNA lysis tubes which contain bashing beads and 1x shield (Zymo Cat. #R1103).

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with homogenization.

  3. Add ZR Bashing beads (Zymo Cat. #S6012-50) to shield sample if not already in lysis tubes. Homogenize the sample using a high speed benchtop homogenizer. Typically, 1 min of vortexing followed by 1 min incubation on ice for a total of 3 cycles will thoroughly homogenize the sample.

  4. Spin the sample to pellet the cell debris. Transfer the supernatant to a new tube.

  5. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen Magbead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Fecal/Soil Microprep Kit.


Tissue/Vectors/Insects:

  1. Completely submerge the tissue sample (<10mg) or vectors into 1x Zymo DNA/RNA Shield. Attempt to break up the sample to allow the shield to penetrate. Alternatively add the tissue sample directly to DNA/RNA Shield lysis tubes which contain bashing beads (Zymo Cat. #R1105).

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with homogenization.

  3. Add sterile 5mm ball bearings or ZR BashingBeads (Zymo Cat. #S6014-50) to the sample containing shield unless already in lysis tubes with beads. Homogenize the sample using a high speed benchtop homogenizer or vortexer. Typically 1 min of vortexing followed by a 1 min incubation on ice for a total of 3 rounds will completely homogenize the sample.

  4. Spin the sample to pellet the cell debris. Transfer the supernatant to a new tube.

  5. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen Magbead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Mini or Microprep kit.

Swabs:

  1. Add just enough 1x Zymo DNA/RNA Shield to completely submerge the swab & mix vigorously. Sterile 0.1-0.5mm beads (such as Zymo Bashing Beads https://www.zymoresearch.com/products/zr-bashingbead-lysis-tubes-0-1-0-5-mm) can be added to the sample and vortexed to aid in the dispersal of microbes from the swab into the shield solution.

  2. Supernatant from the swab sample or swab frozen in shield can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  3. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen Magbead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep


Isolates:

  1. Using a sterile pipet tip, scoop a small portion of the bacterial or fungal isolate and mix directly into 1x Zymo DNA/RNA Shield. Vortex briefly. Alternatively add isolate sample directly to DNA/RNA lysis tubes which contain bashing beads and 1x shield (Zymo Cat. #R1103)

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with homogenization.

  3. Add ZR Bashing beads (Zymo Cat. #S6012-50) to shield sample if not already in lysis tubes. Homogenize the sample using a high speed benchtop homogenizer. Typically 1 min of vortexing followed by 1 min incubation on ice for a total of 3 rounds will homogenize the sample thoroughly.

  4. Spin the sample to pellet the cell debris. Transfer the supernatant to a new tube.

  5. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen Magbeads, Zymo Fungal/Bacterial Zymobiomics DNA/RNA Miniprep


Urine:

  1. Collect urine in a sterile collection tube. Add 70uL of Zymo urine conditioning buffer (Zymo Cat. #D3061-1-8) for each 1mL of urine. Mix by inverting several times.

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  3. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit


Whole Blood:

  1. Suggested starting volume 25-50uL. Be aware that the blood will clot! Add 2 volumes of 2x DNA/RNA shield to the whole blood sample. Mix vigorously by vortexing.

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  3. Perform a proteinase K treatment (8uL of 20mg/mL Proteinase K solution for every 400uL of sample in shield) and mix the sample until the sample is no longer clotting, incubate for 5-15 minutes at RT, mixing occasionally.

  4. Spin the sample to pellet the cell debris. Transfer the supernatant to a new tube.

  5. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Blood tube kit, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep or Miniprep kit.


Plasma/Serum:

  1. Suggested starting volume >200uL. Add 1 volume 2x DNA/RNA shield to the plasma or serum sample. Mix by inverting several times.

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  3. Perform a proteinase K treatment (4uL of 20mg/mL Proteinase K solution for every 400uL of sample in shield) and mix the sample. Incubate at RT for 5-10 min, mixing occasionally.

  4. Spin the sample to pellet the cell debris. Transfer the supernatant to a new tube.

  5. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit


Saliva, tracheal aspirates, bronchial lavage:

  1. Suggested starting volume >100uL. Add 1 volume 2x DNA/RNA shield to the saliva, tracheal aspirates, or bronchial lavage sample. Mix by inverting several times.

  2. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  3. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit


CSF:

Raw CSF, no preservative added

  1. Suggested starting volume of raw CSF: >200uL

  2. Ideally begin with raw, unfrozen specimen. If already frozen, remove CSF specimen (containing no preservative) from -80°C and thaw on ice

  3. Spin 100-200uL sample aliquot at 1500xg for 5min

  4. Save supernatant [sequence this sample] and save pellet [save for backup], in two different tubes

  5. Add DNA/RNA Shield

    1. Supernatant sample: add 1 volume 2x DNA/RNA Shield to 1 volume supernatant

    2. Pellet sample: add ~100-200uL 1x DNA/RNA Shield to the pellet

  6. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions of the supernatant

  7. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit


CSF in preservative such as DNA/RNA Shield

  1. Suggested starting volume of CSF-preservative mix: >400uL

  2. Remove CSF samples (containing preservative) from -80°C and thaw sample on ice

  3. Spin 200-400uL aliquot at 1500xg for 5min

  4. Save supernatant [sequence this sample] and save pellet [save for backup], in two different tubes

    1. Add ~100uL 1x DNA/RNA Shield to the pellet

  5. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions of the supernatant

  6. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit


Sputum:

  1. Suggested collection volume > 1 mL. Beware that sputum is viscous and can be tricky to aliquot, so ideally collect a dedicated sample for mNGS.

  2. Add 1:1 volume of 2x DNA/RNA Shield immediately upon collection. One option is to use these Zymo Saliva/Sputum collection tubes. Another option is to pre-aliquot Shield into tubes, and pour it into the sputum sample immediately upon collection (Note: DNA/RNA Shield contains guanidinium salts and so needs to be handled carefully)

  3. Shake the tubes well to ensure Shield is well mixed with the sputum sample.

  4. Samples can be frozen at -80°C or proceed with extractions.

  5. In case samples are too viscous to pipet, consider digesting the sample to break down viscosity (e.g. using collagenase)

  6. Purify with one of the following: Zymo Pathogen MagBead, Zymo Quick DNA/RNA Microprep kit

2. Nucleic Acid Extractions


Goal: To extract high quality DNA and/or RNA


Workflow:

  • Zymo kits (Quick-DNA/RNA kit, Pathogen MagBead, ZymoBiomics Kits)

  • Qiagen kits (AllPrep, RNAeasy, QIAmp) or other commercial extraction kits (Promega, Biomerieux) also will work fine if Zymo kits are not available.


Considerations:

  • Most commercial kits that are certified DNAse/RNAse free from Zymo or Qiagen are typically good enough for mNGS. We prefer Zymo kits because we’ve found that they have a better yield than the Qiagen kits.

  • We typically use the Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Microprep or Zymo Pathogen Magbead kits

  • For whole blood, using the general kits (like Microprep or Pathogen Magbead), samples must be pre-treated, see vendor protocol.

  • For plasma/sera, perform a proteinase K treatment to avoid clogging columns


Protocols:

  1. For RNA/DNA combined extractions:

    1. Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Microprep Plus kit (column based): best for low concentration samples

    2. Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Miniprep Plus kit (column based): best for high concentration samples

    3. Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Pathogen MagBead kit (bead based): best to do many samples simultaneously, must have magnetic stand that can hold 1.5mL tubes or 96 well magnetic stand.

    4. Zymobiomics DNA/RNA Miniprep kit (column based): good for microbiome samples for the most even lysis of microbes, microcentrifuge and homogenizer required

    5. Qiagen AllPrep DNA/RNA Micro kit (column based): best for low concentration samples

    6. Qiagen AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini kit (column based): best for high concentration samples


  1. For DNA extractions only:

    1. Zymo Quick-DNA Fecal/Soil kits (column and bead based kits): Both column and bead based kits depending on the availability of a microcentrifuge or magnetic stands. Fecal and soil samples typically extract poorly in non-specific kits (i.e.: Zymo Quick-DNA/RNA Micro or Mini prep kit) because of inhibitors in the samples.

    2. Zymo Quick-DNA Fungal/Bacterial kits (column based): for isolates/colonies

    3. Zymo Quick-DNA Urine Kit (column based)


  1. For RNA extractions only:

    1. Zymo Quick-RNA Microprep Plus kit (column based): best for low concentration samples.

    2. Zymo Quick-RNA Miniprep Plus kit (column based): best for high concentration samples

    3. Zymo Quick-RNA Fecal/Soil Microbe kit (column based)

    4. Zymo Quick-RNA Fungal Bacterial kit (column based): for isolates/colonies

    5. Qiagen RNeasy mini kit (column based)

3. Nucleic Acid Quality Control (QC)


Goal: Quantify and Qualitatively assess the amount and integrity of input nucleic acids prior to starting library preparation


Workflow: Assess the nucleic acid concentration and quality before library preparation.


Considerations:

  • Quantification can be performed using a Qubit, Nanodrop, Agilent Bioanalyzer, Agilent Tapestation, Agilent Fragment Analyzer, Qiagen Qiaxcel. Qubit results are much highly preferred over the nanodrop. Capillary systems (bioanalyzer, tapestation, qiaxcel, etc) are preferred over the qubit.

  • Quality control can be performed using any of the capillary electrophoresis systems or, if you have high quantities of sample input, a standard agarose gel can also be used. The aim of quality control is to know the integrity of either RNA (by proxy rRNA bands) or DNA (high molecular weight bands) depending on whether you have extracted either DNA or RNA.


Protocol:

Follow the manufacturer’s protocol specific to either RNA or DNA.

DNA quantification by Qubit HS DNA kit

4a. Illumina Library Preparation - RNA Libraries


There are many commercially available library preparation kits, which fall into two main workflows: ligation and tagmentation. Below we include detailed methods for using the NEBNext Ultra II kit (ligation-based) with minor adaptations.


Many commercially available library preparation kits may be suitable for metagenomic libraries. If trying another library preparation kit, we recommend the following adaptations:

  • Addition of ERCCs at the start of library preparation (internal control)

  • Addition of Qiagen FastSelect to reduce host rRNA

  • Use of truncated Mini Adapters at 3uM (see Appendix K, for ligation-based kits)

  • Reduction of reaction volume to ½ or ¼ of prescribed volumes (for cost-saving)


Considerations:

  • All steps to be done under a sterile clean hood until NEB PCR ENRICHMENT STEP; then everything can be done on the bench.

  • Reactions should be done in sterile RNAse/ DNAse/ DNA-free tubes.

5. Library QC & Pooling


Goal: Determine the quality of the finished libraries, pool libraries and quantify the final pooled libraries


Workflow: High sensitivity DNA Qubit, Tapestation/Bioanalyzer/Qiaxcel or over-amplified PCR diagnostic + gel electrophoresis, qPCR quantification.


Considerations: Remove all adapter dimers prior to doing a final quantification of the pooled library either by SPRI bead size selection or gel extraction of larger samples. qPCR and/or qubit quantification of the final library are the gold standard for loading onto Illumina sequencers.


Protocol:

See the manufacturer’s protocol for using a capillary electrophoresis quantification system: Tapestation/Bioanalyzer/Fragment Analyzer/Qiaxcel. A Post-PCR decision tree is in the appendix that may be helpful.


  1. Perform a Qubit HS DNA quantification using 1uL of each sample library.

    1. Water controls should have substantially less or unquantifiable concentrations than the rest of the samples.

    2. If one or more sample fails by qubit, you can attempt to perform library amplification. We use the Kapa Real Time Amplification kit (Cat. no KK2701, guide) and only amplify until the library reaches above the standard 2 threshold, removing the sample at the 72C extension step. We would not recommend amplifying beyond 8-10 cycles. SPRI clean the amplified library with a 0.8x SPRI ratio. See Appendix H for Library Amplification SOP.

  2. Assess the sample library sizes by tapestation/bioanalyzer/etc. (this can be done on the final pool rather than each sample individually after you feel confident that you can make consistently sized libraries)

    1. Adaptor dimers show up ~146bp. If adaptor dimers are present, clean up the library using a 0.8x SPRI clean-up. If >10% of the sample is adaptor dimer, it will typically take 2 or even 3 clean-ups before all the adaptor dimers are removed. Alternatively, perform a gel extraction, cutting at approximately 450-800bp in length. After any clean-ups, re-qubit and re-assess library size. This step is critical as adaptor dimers will cluster better on the illumina platform, so even if you only have 5% adaptor dimers in a sample, up to 20% of the final reads will originate from the adaptor dimers.

Appendix I: Right side SPRI size selection


Right side size selection

  1. Thoroughly shake to resuspend the SPRI beads. Add the required volume of SPRIselect for the desired ratio to the sample.

    1. 0.5x for <1000bp

    2. 0.6x for <800bp

    3. 0.7x for <600bp

  2. Mix the total reaction volume by pipetting 10 times and incubate at RT for 5 minutes

  3. Place the tube on the magnetic stand and allow the beads to fully separate, ~2-3 minutes.

  4. Transfer the clear supernatant, which contains the Right Side Size Selected sample, to a new tube. The tube with the remaining beads can be discarded.

NOTE: Care should be taken not to aspirate more than a trace amount of beads during this step as the undesired larger fragment sizes are associated with the beads. Significant bead transfer will cause tailing into the larger size range.

  1. Add the required volume of SPRI beads, using the calculation below, to the supernatant from Step 4 above. This will bind the fragments in the supernatant to the new SPRI beads.

TIP: Sample Volume μL * (1.8x – the initial ratio) = volume of SPRI beads

Example: 50 μL * (1.8 – 0.7) = 55 μL of SPRI

  1. Mix the total reaction volume by pipetting 10 times and incubate at RT for 5 minutes

  2. Place the tube on the magnetic stand and allow the beads to fully separate, ~2-3 minutes.

  3. Remove and discard the clear supernatant.

  4. With the tube still on the magnet, add 180 μL of 80% ethanol and incubate at RT for 30 seconds. Remove and discard the ethanol supernatant.

  5. To elute the sample add ≥ 20 μL of water. Mix the total elution volume by pipetting 10 times to resuspend the beads and incubate at RT for 5 minutes

  6. Place the tube on an appropriate magnetic stand or plate and allow the SPRI beads to settle to the magnet. Settle times will vary; a higher elution volume or weaker magnets will require a longer settle time.

  7. Transfer the eluate (size selected sample) to an appropriate storage vessel.

Appendix K: TruSeq-compatible mini adapters


For complete information, please see the Mini adapter info sheet

Background and introduction

Adapter dimer is an unwanted byproduct of library preparation, especially when working with low input samples. Size-specific SPRI bead cleanups are used to remove the dimers which are typically smaller than the target library size (<200bp). However, dimer can persist which interferes with sequencing output and quality.


To address this issue of adapter dimer, we share new TruSeq-compatible mini adapters. These adapters can swap into the current Biohub mNGS library preparation protocol (and others) with minor modifications and are compatible with TruSeq-based indexing primers.


The adapters are shorter than the typical NEB/Illumina adapters so they can be used at higher concentrations and still be removed efficiently with a single SPRI bead clean after barcoding PCR. Mini adapter dimer is ~50bp whereas traditional adapter dimer is ~150bp.


Based on our pilot, we recommend piloting these mini adapters and implementing in place of the NEB adapters.
